## ꒰ 🦢 ꒱ Business Rules

The monthly fee multiplier varies according to the client's age:

| 🌷 Age Bracket | 🎀 Rate Multiplier |
| :--- | :---: |
| 0 to 18 years | `1.0x` *(Base rate)* |
| 19 to 28 years | `1.5x` |
| 29 to 38 years | `2.25x` |
| 39 to 48 years | `2.4x` |
| 49 to 58 years | `3.5x` |
| 59 years or older | `6.0x` |
